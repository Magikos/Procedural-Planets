## Behavior and ownership

FishingReview.unity supports cast, wait, nibble, bite, pull, catch, and recovery. E starts at the fishing stance. E during the two-second bite window hooks the fish. Escape cancels. Missing or inactive water target and unexpected movement cancel the interaction. Recovery returns movement authority.

ActorFishing owns decisions around ActorInteractionSession. A valid catch marker awards exactly one fish through the existing InventoryService. Missed bites, early input, cancellation before the marker, and target loss do not award fish. Repeated cancel does not restart recovery. This inventory belongs to the fixture; main-game integration remains open.

FishingInteractionReview connects the existing actor callbacks, performance player, prop graph, and HeldToolGrip. The primary palm owns rod placement. The supporting palm uses the shared ProceduralPoseRig. Its correction fades between 4 and 12 centimetres of error, preserving authored rotation. There is no new pose writer or per-animation authoring generator.
