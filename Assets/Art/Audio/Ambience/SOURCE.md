# Ambience audio

Nineteen `.wav` files, one `.mp3`, and `catalog.json`.

`catalog.json` is the provenance record for this folder and is more detailed than
this file: it carries, per clip, the title, category, the exact source path, the
licence note, and what to listen for when reviewing it. Read it before changing
anything here.

Sources, per that catalog: most clips come from packs in D:/Unity/Explore Assets — Procedural
Worlds Ambient Sounds, UniStorm Weather System, Farm Animal Sounds, SurfaceData,
and Fantacode Swimming System. `cicada-pixabay.mp3` came from
https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/ instead.

Seven entries in the catalog still read "Not selected yet" — those slots have no
chosen source.

Licensing: the catalog marks the pack-sourced clips "Owned Asset Store pack. Verify
included audio terms before production use." That verification has not been done.

Not imported: no pack scripts, mixers, or demo scenes. The files were copied in;
they carry no `AssetOrigin` metadata.
