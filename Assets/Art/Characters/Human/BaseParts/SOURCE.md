# Modular body parts

Source: D:/Unity/Explore Assets/Assets/Synty/SidekickCharacters/
Pack: Synty Sidekick Characters

Twenty-two `SK_HUMN_BASE_01_*` meshes: head, nose, teeth, tongue, eyebrows, eyes,
ears, torso, upper and lower arms, hands, hips, legs and feet. Together they are the modular human body
the kit assembles.

They were copied in by hand, which is why they carry no `AssetOrigin` metadata.

Open: these files still carry the vendor filename. They have not been renamed to
our `Thing_NN` convention yet, and nineteen C# references point at the vendor names.
Renaming them means editing those references in the same pass.

Not imported: no vendor scripts, shaders, creator tools, or character-builder UI.
