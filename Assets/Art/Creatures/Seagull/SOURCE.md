# Seagull audio

`SeagullCall1.ogg` and `SeagullCall2.ogg` are the two call clips for the seagull
species. `SeagullAudio.asset` is ours — `CreatureBirdAssetAuthor` creates it and
points it at these two clips.

Provenance for the two `.ogg` files is not recorded. They carry no `AssetOrigin`
metadata, they are untracked in git, and no design document names them. Re-establish
where they came from before production use.

The seagull's mesh and clips are not here; the bird visuals are built by
`CreatureBirdAssetAuthor` from the shared bird source.
