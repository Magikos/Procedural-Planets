# Terrain ground materials

All eighteen are ours. Nothing here came from a pack.

One material per biome surface (beach, cave, desert, forest, grassland, ice bog,
mountain, ocean, savanna, scrub, snow, steppe, swamp and the rest), plus
`Ground_LodStrip.mat` for the distant LOD strip. All use URP Lit.

They are bound by the terrain chunk system, not by a scatter prototype, which is
why they sit apart from the scatter materials one level up.
