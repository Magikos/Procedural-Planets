Bryan, 2026-09-03: "Sometimes your responses are still a bit verbose and overwhelming, I get
lost on what was actually done with all the details and especially if there is something asked
of me, I get lost in what needs to be done."

**Default response shape:**

```
**Done**
- <one line per thing that changed>

**You**
1. <action item>   (or: nothing)
```

Nothing else. No measurement tables, no code blocks, no rationale, no "why this design",
no known-limits section — unless he asks. He expands with "details", "why", or a question.

**Why:** the action items were drowning in evidence. He could not tell what had changed from
what was being explained, and could not find what was being asked of him. Long responses were
costing him the decision, not informing it.

**How to apply:**
- Action items go in their own section with numbers, never buried in prose.
- If nothing is needed from him, write "nothing" — do not leave him hunting.
- One line per completed thing. File links are fine; explanations are not.
- Findings and evidence are held back until asked. They are not deleted from the work, just
  from the response.
- Applies on top of [[feedback-quality-over-cheap]] — brevity governs the REPORT, never the
  work. Still take the root-cause fix; just say less about it.
- An explicitly requested report, plan or walkthrough is exempt. That is him asking.
